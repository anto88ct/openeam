Frequently asked questions

Is the evolution of the OpenEAM platform included in the investment?

Absolutely. We believe in a long-term partnership. All updates, new features, and security improvements released for your active modules are included in your subscription, so you always have the most advanced technology available with no hidden costs.

What investment is required for the initial setup phase?

Setup isn't a fixed cost — it's a tailored path. It includes our Digital Maturity Audit and configuring the platform around your processes. The investment depends on complexity and active modules, and it's calibrated to be sustainable and to generate measurable savings within the first few weeks, quickly covering the initial outlay.

Are there discounts for non-profit organizations or special projects?

Yes, OpenEAM supports efficiency in the non-profit sector too. We evaluate dedicated, discounted investment plans for non-profit organizations and social-impact projects, subject to eligibility checks.

How flexible is the contractual commitment with OpenEAM?

Our philosophy is value-based: we want you to stay with us because OpenEAM saves you money, not because of a contractual lock-in. For the Cloud (SaaS) model, we offer maximum flexibility to adjust or cancel your plan. You keep full control of the solution, and data export is always guaranteed.

How does OpenEAM adapt if my company grows or needs change?

OpenEAM is natively scalable. If your team grows or you need to track new processes, you can add users or activate new modules at any time. The investment adjusts proportionally to actual usage, so you always pay only for the value you receive.

How can I be sure OpenEAM will generate real savings for my company?

We don't rely on promises, but on data. During setup, we'll define the KPIs to monitor together. OpenEAM tracks every cost and inefficiency, letting you measure the reduction in direct costs (15-20% on average in the first months) and a fast ROI, often within 6 to 12 months.

My company has very specific processes. Will OpenEAM be able to track and control them?

Yes. Unlike standard software, our strength is customization. Thanks to the initial audit and the platform's modularity, OpenEAM "takes the shape" of your specific processes. This gives you full traceability and audit-proof control, drastically reducing operational risk.

What's the main difference between OpenEAM and the big-name legacy systems?

Agility and a focus on value. While legacy systems require huge investments, long implementation times, and are often rigid, OpenEAM offers an agile Enterprise solution, a fast go-live, and a flexible investment model aimed at quick ROI and granular process control, without the IT maintenance costs of old systems.
