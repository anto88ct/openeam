Seedma: twenty years of know-how, built into software

Founded in 2020 as a spin-off of Teseo Srl, Seedma develops integrated software modules for managing industrial plants, civil infrastructure, and complex facilities. Our Operations & Maintenance know-how, built on more than twenty years of experience in Oil & Gas, translates into innovative solutions that optimize operational processes and improve efficiency, infrastructure management, and business sustainability.

Since 2024 we've been part of the Le Village by Crédit Agricole network.

From operational data to strategic decisions

Our software solutions let you acquire, process, share, and use data from plants and infrastructure to guide strategic decisions. A lean, agile business model that supports companies as their processes evolve.

Consulting and tailored software

We offer our clients software and related consulting services, including assessments, training, and after-sales support. We round out the offering with specialist modules, mobile apps, and dedicated support contracts, configuring every solution around the client's needs.

Experience, flexibility, concrete results

Our mission is to contribute to our clients' success with hands-on field experience, technological expertise, and solutions built around their real needs.

We work alongside organizations operating in complex environments, helping them make their Operations & Maintenance processes clearer, more efficient, and more controllable. We do this with a flexible, modular structure capable of designing, developing, and managing innovative projects with one precise goal: turning complexity into concrete results.
