## The challenge

Managing periodic maintenance at the Augusta (SR), Naples, and Palermo
depots; letting external maintenance contractors file work-order reports;
consolidating multiple legacy software systems covering shift inspections,
fire-safety inspections, and handovers; supporting technical checks at the
marine terminal for incoming vessels.

## The solution

With **Open EAM**, Sonatrach manages periodic maintenance at its depots,
letting external contractors file work-order reports through dedicated
accounts. The platform made it possible to retire **three legacy systems**,
consolidating shift inspections, fire-safety inspections, and handovers into
a single centralized **Inspections** module. Through the **Marine Terminal**
module, Open EAM also supports technical and geometric checks of incoming
vessels and post-mooring inspections — all on one platform.

## The results

- **3 legacy systems** retired and consolidated into a single Inspections
  module
- Maintenance, inspections, and vessel checks coordinated across three depots
- External contractors' work-order reports tracked on the platform
