## The challenge

Coordinating reports, work orders, and maintenance across extensive water
networks spread throughout the Siracusa and Ragusa area, where service
continuity is critical and faults must be found and fixed quickly.

## The solution

SIAM uses **Open EAM** to digitize the management of reports and maintenance
for its aqueducts. The **Reports** module collects and routes work requests,
while scheduled and call-out **Maintenance** is planned and tracked on a
single platform, linked to the network's assets.

## The results

- Reports and work orders centralized and tracked
- Aqueduct maintenance planned on a single platform
