## The challenge

Managing facility maintenance across the two Catania plants (Main and WSiC),
with over **40,000 work orders** a year carried out by both in-house
maintenance staff and external contractors. The goal: keep contracts,
planning, and spare-parts/price-list usage under control, and organize the
technical documentation linked to assets so field technicians can find it
quickly.

## The solution

STMicroelectronics manages facility maintenance across both plants on **Open
EAM**. Monitoring maintenance contracts keeps planning and spare-parts/price-
list usage under control. The documents module organizes the technical
documentation linked to assets, letting maintenance staff retrieve it in
seconds.

## The results

- Over **40,000 maintenance work orders** handled every year
- More than **80%** planned activity versus call-out work
- **10%** savings achieved on maintenance contract renewals
