## The challenge

Coordinating maintenance, asset management, and technical documentation in a
single environment for complex, highly critical refining plants, where
safety and operational continuity are top priorities.

## The solution

SARAS adopted **Open EAM** to unify maintenance, asset management, and
technical documentation on a single modular platform that adapts to the
plants' existing procedures and operational workflows.

## The results

- Maintenance, assets, and documentation managed on a single platform
- Processes aligned with industry safety standards
