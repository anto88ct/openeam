## The challenge

Managing a huge technical document base and its revision processes; letting
external engineering firms access document revisions without visiting the
physical on-site archive; ensuring version consistency for control-room
procedures; running fire-safety inspection processes on critical assets.

## The solution

Thanks to Open EAM's flexible workflows, Sasol manages technical documents and
revision processes through the **Technical Archive** module. External
engineering firms can check out, download, and upload revisions directly
from Open EAM, with no need to access the physical archive. Documents and
assets are linked through the **Operating Manuals** module, enabling
control-room procedure lookup and version consistency. Sasol also manages
fire-safety inspection processes on critical assets — all in a single system.

## The results

- Over **60,000 technical documents** and their revision processes managed
  through the Technical Archive module
- Remote, tracked access to revisions for external engineering firms
- Centralized fire-safety inspections and control-room procedures
