## The challenge

Third-party inspections, scheduled maintenance, and call-out work orders were
hard to manage; engineering project documentation lived in a disorganized
folder system; non-technical users had no way to open maintenance requests.

## The solution

SAC redesigned its maintenance plan on **Open EAM**, optimizing the
management of third-party inspections, scheduled maintenance, and call-out
work orders. The **Reports** app also lets non-technical users open
maintenance tickets. Engineering project documentation moved from
disorganized folders to a structured approach with unique codes and full
traceability through the **Technical Archive**. While managing work orders,
operators access technical documents and layouts linked to the relevant
assets — all on a single platform.

## The results

- Third-party inspections, scheduled maintenance, and call-out work orders
  managed in a single system
- Maintenance tickets opened even by non-technical users through Reports
- Structured project documentation, with unique codes and full traceability
