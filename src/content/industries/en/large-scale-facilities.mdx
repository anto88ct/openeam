      cost, eliminates system fragmentation, and improves your ability to
      respond to unforeseen events and emergencies.

    body: >-

      searchable, always up-to-date system. Documentation is linked directly
      to the relevant assets, ensuring immediate access to the information you
      need. This cuts response times, increases efficiency, and minimizes the
      risk of non-compliance during inspections or audits.
  - title: >-

    icon: chart
    body: >-

      allocation, and coordinate work teams in real time, wherever they are.
  - title: >-

    icon: labyrinth
    body: >-

      continuity.
