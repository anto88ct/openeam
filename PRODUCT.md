# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Ops/maintenance managers and plant engineers at industrial companies (manufacturing, oil & gas, large-scale facilities) evaluating EAM/CMMS software. They're comparing Open EAM against rigid legacy EAM/CMMS incumbents and want to see the platform adapts to their processes, not vice versa.

## Product Purpose

Open EAM unifies maintenance, asset management, technical documentation and analytics in one modular, customizable platform for industrial plants. It exists to replace rigid legacy EAM tools that force companies into fixed workflows. Success = visitor requests a demo or watches the platform video tour.

## Brand Personality

Challenger-brand, informal ("tu"), benefit-led, provocative toward legacy EAM/CMMS incumbents. Confident and direct, not corporate-stiff. Tagline: "Il primo software di gestione asset e manutenzione che si adatta ai tuoi processi. E non viceversa."

## Anti-references

Legacy enterprise EAM/CMMS software: dense, generic-corporate, rigid-looking UI marketing (IBM Maximo, SAP-style enterprise brochureware). Avoid stiff, jargon-heavy "enterprise-grade" marketing tone and bland corporate blue/gray palettes.

## Design Principles

- Adapts to you, not the other way around: every section should reinforce flexibility/modularity over rigid legacy tooling.
- Challenger confidence: direct, benefit-led copy, no corporate hedging.
- Proof over claims: named client logos (STMicroelectronics, Sonatrach, SASOL...), testimonials, case studies carry credibility.
- One consistent system: canonical brand tokens (orange/teal/ink) and Poppins/Inter typography applied uniformly across pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

`prefers-reduced-motion` already respected globally (src/styles/global.css). No other WCAG level specified; maintain ≥4.5:1 body text contrast and visible focus rings (already in place via `:focus-visible`).
